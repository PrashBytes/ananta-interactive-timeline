# ANANTA — An Interactive Journey Through Indian Art History

> **An immersive digital timeline exploring the evolution of Indian art through time, artifacts, culture, and storytelling.**

## 📖 About the Project

**ANANTA** is an interactive web-based timeline designed to explore the rich and diverse history of Indian art.

Instead of presenting art history as a conventional list of dates and facts, ANANTA transforms it into an immersive visual journey. Users can scroll through major periods of Indian art history, discover significant artifacts, and interact with individual artworks to learn about their historical and artistic significance.

The project combines **interactive storytelling, modern web design, animations, and historical content** to create a digital museum-like experience.

## 🏛️ Historical Periods Covered

### 01 — Indus Valley Civilization
**c. 2500 BCE**

Explores early Indian artistic expression through bronze sculptures, seals, terracotta objects, and other archaeological artifacts.

**Featured Artifact:**  
- Dancing Girl of Mohenjo-daro

---

### 02 — Buddhist & Classical India
**c. 200 BCE – 600 CE**

Explores Buddhist art, cave paintings, sculptures, and narrative traditions.

**Featured Examples:**  
- Ajanta Cave Paintings
- Sanchi Art
- Gandhara / Mathura Sculpture

---

### 03 — Temple & Medieval Art
**c. 600 – 1200 CE**

Explores temple sculpture, architecture, bronze casting, and religious artistic traditions.

**Featured Artifact:**  
- Chola Bronze Nataraja

---

### 04 — Mughal & Courtly Art
**c. 1500 – 1800 CE**

Explores miniature painting, illustrated manuscripts, royal portraiture, and courtly artistic traditions.

**Featured Examples:**  
- Mughal Miniature Paintings
- Illustrated Manuscripts
- Court Paintings

---

### 05 — Regional Painting Traditions

Explores the diversity of India's regional artistic traditions.

**Featured Styles:**

- Madhubani
- Warli
- Kalamkari
- Pattachitra
- Rajasthani Miniature Painting
- Pahari Painting
- Tanjore Painting

---

### 06 — Colonial & Transitional India
**c. 1800 – 1947**

Explores the interaction between traditional Indian artistic practices and Western academic influences.

**Featured Artists / Movements:**

- Raja Ravi Varma
- Company Painting
- Bengal School

---

### 07 — Modern & Contemporary India
**1947 – Present**

Explores the transformation of Indian art after independence and the emergence of modern and contemporary artistic practices.

**Featured Artists:**

- Amrita Sher-Gil
- M. F. Husain
- Contemporary Indian Artists

## 🎨 Design Concept

ANANTA is inspired by the visual language of premium digital experiences and interactive exhibitions.

The design focuses on:

- Cinematic storytelling
- Large editorial typography
- Full-screen visual sections
- Scroll-based transitions
- Layered imagery
- Minimal navigation
- Interactive artifacts
- Historical visual themes

Each historical period has its own visual atmosphere while maintaining a consistent overall design language.
